## Projects

**[Home Credit: credit-risk scorecard and approval strategy](https://github.com/Anant3623/home-credit-risk)** · [Live dashboard](https://anant3623.github.io/home-credit-risk/dashboard/)
PostgreSQL pipeline over 58M rows of loan and repayment history, a logistic scorecard with a test AUC of 0.751, and an approval simulator. Approving 55% of applicants instead of 50% raises the bad rate from 2.92% to 3.21%, but the added applicants default at about 6%.

**[Olist: late deliveries and customer reviews](https://github.com/Anant3623/olist-delivery-reviews)** · [Live dashboard](https://anant3623.github.io/olist-delivery-reviews/dashboard/)
96K delivered e-commerce orders. Late orders average 2.27 stars against 4.29 on time, and most of the delay happens in carrier transit, not at the seller.

**[Olist: first-order retention](https://github.com/Anant3623/first-order-retention-analysis)**
Why 98% of Olist customers never return: product category matters about six times more than delivery speed as a retention signal.
